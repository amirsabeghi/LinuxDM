Setup & supported links

Install the desktop bridge once from your LinuxDM project folder:

python3 -m linuxdm.firefox_install

Play a video to detect its page or stream, then choose Download above. Video downloads use yt-dlp and FFmpeg on your computer. Choose a named queue and quality in the desktop dialog.

Browser cookies and signed-in sessions are not transferred. DRM-protected videos are not supported.

Automatic capture is on by default. LinuxDM takes over HTTP/HTTPS downloads after accepting them. If the handoff fails, the download stays in Firefox. Turn the switch off to use Firefox for a download.

Private downloads, browser-generated files (blob/data URLs), and downloads flagged by Firefox stay in Firefox. Session-dependent downloads may also need Firefox.
